About Us

SIFT Bearing Store supplies imported rolling bearings and provides selection support. We digitised the original manufacturer catalogues of NSK, Timken and FAG (Schaeffler) into this searchable database so that engineers and buyers can check specifications, find interchanges and compare options in seconds.

Where the data comes from

Every record is parsed line by line from the manufacturers' published catalogue PDFs, and each one keeps the catalogue page number so any value can be traced back to the source:

  • NSK — inch product catalogue and the metric catalogue CAT. No. E1103
  • Timken — Tapered Roller Bearing Catalog (2016) and Bearing Dimension Catalog
  • FAG / Schaeffler — Rolling Bearings catalogue HR 1

The parsed data was cross-checked between catalogues: for part numbers that appear in more than one source the bore diameters agree to within 0.001 mm, and over 95% of the weights match within ±15% (the remainder comes from pound/kilogram rounding in the original tables). Weights are normalised to kg; load ratings keep both the original N and lbs values.
